RISE

Music that keeps pace with your workout

Songs don't know when you're working hard. The chorus ends just as your set starts, or kicks in while you rest. RISE rearranges the song as you go, using jump cuts only: it loops the high-energy part while you work and skips ahead through it when you rest, cutting only at points where the music repeats so you don't hear the seam.

  2. 2Steer it: L loop, S skip, N normal · click the timeline or ← → to move
  3. 3The athlete squats while the music is high energy

How it works

  1. Sections. Each song is split into sections and labelled high or low intensity by drum loudness (within 5 dB of the loudest).
  2. Cutpoints. Pairs of beats inside one section where the music repeats, so jumping between them is seamless.
  3. Loop takes the longest jump back in the current section; Skip the longest jump forward. Nothing ever jumps between sections.
  4. The athlete squats on the beat while the music is high energy and sits down to rest when it calms.

Sections and cutpoints are precomputed offline. The audio is never filtered or time-stretched.

Web re-implementation of “RISE: Adaptive Music Playback for Realtime Intensity Synchronization with Exercise” (Wang, Donahue & Jain, ISMIR 2025), unguided mode. Songs are copyrighted and for local research use only.
