MARingBA

Music-Adaptive Ringtones for Blended Audio notifications

🏠 Home Story ✦ MARingBA Musical Agents RISE CHI 2024 paper ↗

Your phone rings while music is playing. Normally the music cuts out and the ringtone plays over it. MARingBA plays the ringtone along with the song instead: at the song's tempo, in its key, and starting on the beat.
