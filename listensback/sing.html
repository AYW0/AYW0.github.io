Musical Assistant

Spoken notifications sung into your music

← Ringtones (MARingBA) UIST 2024 paper ↗

When Siri or Alexa talks, your music drops out. A musical assistant sings the message instead, in place of the singer, to the song's beat and the singer's own melody.

How it works

  1. Speak. The message is spoken by a normal text-to-speech voice.
  2. Find syllables. Loudness peaks in the speech mark one syllable each.
  3. Snap to the beat. Each syllable keeps its natural timing, rounded to the nearest 16th note from the next bar.
  4. Borrow the melody. Each syllable takes the pitch the singer sings at that moment, tracked from the vocal stem.
  5. Re-pitch. PSOLA moves each syllable to its note and length, keeping the voice intelligible.
  6. Swap the singer. The original vocals drop out for those bars while the band keeps playing.

The paper generates a new melody with the Anticipatory Music Transformer and aligns speech with a forced aligner; this demo uses the original melody and simple signal processing instead.

Simplified web re-implementation of “Towards Music-Aware Virtual Assistants” (Wang, Lindlbauer & Donahue, UIST 2024). Preset messages use macOS text-to-speech; typed messages use meSpeak (eSpeak, GPL). Songs are copyrighted and for local research use only.
